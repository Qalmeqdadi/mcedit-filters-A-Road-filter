How the purchase works

  • Buyer pays the seller the amount already paid to Radiant plus the agreed premium.
  • Buyer takes over the remaining developer payment plan, mostly due at handover.
  • Transfer completes after Radiant's NOC and registration on DARI (Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre).
  • Registration fee (cited as 2% of the price) and commission are agreed in the MOU.
