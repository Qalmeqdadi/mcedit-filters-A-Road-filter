# WhatsApp messages

> **DRAFT.** In the dashboard, open a broker → *Draft message* → approve → send it yourself → *mark as sent*.
> The broker message templates are built into the dashboard (Approvals tab) and filled in with each
> broker's name. Reach brokers through the enquiry route on their own listing or profile (linked in the
> dashboard). Do not send bulk unsolicited messages: UAE telecom rules and WhatsApp's terms restrict them,
> and it burns goodwill. One personal message per broker, one follow-up at most.

---

## A. To a broker who lists a competing 3BR in the towers (English)

> Hello [Name], I'm the owner of a 3BR in Radiant Marina Towers. I saw your 3BR listing at AED [their price] in the same towers.
> If you have buyers who liked the building but not that unit, mine may suit them: [SIZE] sqft, [VIEW], [FLOOR] floor, about [PAID %] of the plan paid. Asking AED [PRICE]. I'm happy to co-broke at the standard 2%. Shall I send the floor plan?

## C. Reply to an inbound enquiry (qualifying questions)

> Thanks for your interest. A few quick questions so I can send the right documents:
> 1. Are you buying to live in or as an investment?
> 2. Is the upfront amount (AED [PAID + PREMIUM]) available in cash, and how do you plan to fund the balance at handover?
> 3. What timeframe are you working to?
> 4. Are you working with a broker?
> Then I'll send the floor plan, the payment schedule and a viewing of the show unit or site.

## D. Follow-up (once, after 5 days without a reply)

> Hello [Name], just following up on the Radiant Marina 3BR (AED [PRICE]). Happy to send the floor plan whenever useful. If it's not a fit, no problem at all.
